About

A calm, thorough approach to children’s heart health.

I am a consultant paediatrician with expertise in cardiology. I see children with heart murmurs, suspected congenital heart disease, palpitations, chest pain, and other cardiac concerns, as well as wider paediatric presentations.

Appointments are unhurried. I explain findings in plain language, discuss next steps clearly, and work with families, general practitioners, and specialist teams so that care stays joined up.

Clinical interests

Paediatric cardiology

Congenital echocardiography

General paediatrics

Consultant-level care for children’s wider health, with clear explanations and thoughtful follow-up for families.
